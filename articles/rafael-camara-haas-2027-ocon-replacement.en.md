## What happened

Haas announced on October 15 that Rafael Camara will drive for the team from 2027 on a multi-year deal, taking the seat Esteban Ocon vacates at the end of this season. Camara is 21, Brazilian and a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy. He won the Formula Regional European Championship in 2024 and the FIA Formula 3 title in 2025, and in his rookie Formula 2 season he leads the standings by seven points over Nikola Tsolov with two rounds left, according to Formula1.com.

He has never started a Formula 1 race. Ocon, who confirmed his exit on September 30 and told RACER it is "absolutely not the end of my F1 career," leaves with 197 starts, one win (Hungary 2021) and four podiums in PaddockIntel's database.

## Why it happened

Haas decided it on a stopwatch. In August, the team ran four candidates in previous-generation cars at Portimão: Camara, reigning F2 champion Leonardo Fornaroli, Haas reserve Jack Doohan and Toyota-backed reserve Ryo Hirakawa. "He was the quickest. That was a very simple answer," team principal Ayao Komatsu said, as reported by Crash.net, calling performance "the fully overriding factor above everything else." On the Ferrari link, Komatsu said the relationship "gave us access to the FDA drivers," not the decision.

He had set that rule in public before the test result was known. In September he told RacingNews365 that affiliations or backing only count when two drivers are equally fast, and that Haas would not take a driver three-tenths slower because of Toyota or Ferrari links or an extra five million.

Haas also has its own recent evidence for betting on an academy rookie over a veteran. Oliver Bearman, the previous Ferrari junior Haas promoted, outscored Ocon 41-38 in his rookie season in 2025 and out-qualified him 14-10. In 2026 the gap widened: 23 points to 7 after Singapore, and 12-4 in qualifying.

## Economic impact

The money in this decision sits mostly outside the cost cap. The FIA excludes an F1 driver's pay, travel and accommodation from the $215 million limit, so what Haas paid Ocon or will pay Camara comes out of the team's budget without changing its capped spending. For Haas that budget line matters more than for most: The Race reported the team operates below the cost cap and is "generally underfunded," and that it "couldn't justify paying" Ocon "what is understood to be a multi-million dollar salary" against his results. Haas has not disclosed Camara's terms.

Two sources of support come with him. Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur said in August that Ferrari provides Camara "financial backing" and has run "many TPC tests with him this year," and The Race reported commercial benefits from his personal Brazilian backers. Neither has published a figure.

The third is the market. Gabriel Bortoleto is contracted at Audi for 2027, according to Audi team principal Mattia Binotto, so next season would be Brazil's first with two drivers on the grid since 2016, when Felipe Massa and Felipe Nasr both raced. That is a commercial asset Ocon could not bring, even though Komatsu says it did not pick the driver.

## The framework

Haas now runs the same pipeline twice: a Ferrari academy driver promoted into a Haas race seat. With Bearman it worked on the numbers within one season. With Camara the test was decisive, but a test is not a Grand Prix weekend. Komatsu praised his qualifying at Monaco and Montreal, and The Race noted that a mistake cost him a Monaco win and another proved costly in Montreal.

This is a correction of the story we told on September 30, not just a sequel. That piece treated Camara as already confirmed and gave him a backing figure its sources didn't contain; it now carries a dated correction. ## Verdict

My read: Haas made the cheaper choice, and on its own data it is also the more defensible one. A rookie with no F1 starts is a real risk, but the only head-to-head Haas has run between an academy rookie and Ocon went the rookie's way in both seasons, and this time the team had a four-driver test to back the call. The money and the Brazilian market are bonuses the team will gladly take. The decision was made at Portimão.
