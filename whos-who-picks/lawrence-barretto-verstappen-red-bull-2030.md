Lawrence Barretto on Max Verstappen extending with Red Bull until at least the end of
2030, announced ahead of his home Dutch GP. Verified independently: his previous deal
ran through 2028 but carried performance-based exit clauses that had fueled 18 months
of departure rumors — Verstappen had even hinted at retiring earlier in the year over
his frustration with the current car generation. This new deal removes that
uncertainty; by 2030 he'll have spent 15 seasons at Red Bull. Barretto's own read —
"more dominoes fall now, with all the big seats locked out for 2027" — is the same
seat-scarcity pattern the Nate Saunders pick already flagged for Norris/McLaren: two
independent reporters converging on the same real structural shift in the driver
market.
