David Hayhoe on a genuinely obscure front-row stat, posted the same day as the 2021
Russian GP: that race was the first since the 2008 British GP where neither front-row
starter had ever won a race before — and before that, you have to go back to Sweden 1975 for the previous time
the entire top three on the grid were still winless. He cites his own reference,
section 535 of "Formula 1 The Knowledge" — the kind of primary-source rigor that comes
from having compiled the *Grand Prix Data Book* across four editions since 1991. It's
exactly the sort of statistic that's easy to state and hard to verify without a
complete, accurate results database going back decades — the same discipline
PaddockIntel applies to its own historical data.
