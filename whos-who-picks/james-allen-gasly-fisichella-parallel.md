James Allen drawing a real historical parallel after Pierre Gasly kept the points and
trophy for his 2026 Monaco GP P3 but missed the actual podium moment: the same thing
happened once before for an outright win. Giancarlo Fisichella was awarded the 2003
Brazilian GP win after the race, once officials established the red flag had come out
a lap later than the timing system first showed — Kimi Räikkönen had already stood on
the top step and taken the trophy. F1 tried to make it right with a trophy handover at
the following race. Allen isn't relaying this secondhand — he was commentating that
2003 race live for ITV and says he remembers watching the timing screens change in
real time. Worth flagging: this post is from June, after the first (stewards')
reversal restored Gasly's P3 — before September's FIA Court of Appeal ruling (see the
Medland and Cooper picks) stripped him of the position entirely. The historical
parallel about missing your own podium moment stands regardless of how the underlying
case ultimately resolved.
