Scott Mitchell-Malm on F1 CEO Stefano Domenicali's real, controversial comment
justifying the removal of battery-usage graphics from race broadcasts (missing at
Silverstone and Spa): "no one is interested in how you drive your car," citing a
conversation with Star Wars director George Lucas — a genuine F1 fan who attends
several races a paddock-side each year — who told him new fans care about "action,"
not throttle angle or brake percentage. Verified independently: the comment sparked
real backlash from technically-engaged fans who felt dismissed. Mitchell-Malm's own
framing — "another disappointing but unsurprising stance," pointing to a sharper
takedown by a colleague — captures a genuine tension in how F1's business leadership
thinks about its audience: is the sport's growth built on hiding the technical layer,
or explaining it? That tension is the exact reason PaddockIntel exists.
