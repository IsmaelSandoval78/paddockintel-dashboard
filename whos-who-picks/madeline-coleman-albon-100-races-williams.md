Madeline Coleman on Alex Albon's 100th race start for Williams — his own words carrying
the piece: "I want this to go well. I poured a lot of my time and energy into this
team, and I hope it's for something." Verified independently: Albon became the first
driver in Williams' history to reach 100 starts for the team (Hungary), passing Nigel
Mansell's team appearance record along the way after Toro Rosso, a promoted Red Bull
seat lasting barely a season, and a year out in DTM before Williams took him on in
2022. Fewer than 30 drivers in F1 history have reached a century of starts with a
single constructor — a real loyalty/rebuild story, not a stat for its own sake.
