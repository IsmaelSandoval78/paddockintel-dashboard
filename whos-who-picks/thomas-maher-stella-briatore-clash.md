Thomas Maher relaying McLaren team principal Andrea Stella's on-the-record response to
Flavio Briatore at the Monza press conference, in the fallout from the same Monaco GP
Court of Appeal ruling covered by the Medland, Cooper, and Allen picks. Verified with
external sources: Briatore had accused one of the FIA judges of an undisclosed personal
connection to McLaren, pointing to a photo of the judge at a 2018 McLaren-sponsored
event and a claim involving McLaren cars donated to a foundation. Stella's response, in
full: "I find that this conference going in this direction is quite insulting for
McLaren, for the reputation of a team that deserves a lot of respect. And if anybody
makes these kind of allegations — which seem quite serious — they are going to have to
take the responsibility for what they are saying in a public forum. The hearing, the
whole International Court of Appeal, was conducted to the highest standards and we
should not question this phase like it's been done." A direct, on-record team-principal
clash over the integrity of the sport's own judicial process — not a driving dispute,
a governance one.
