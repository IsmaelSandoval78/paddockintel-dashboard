Nate Saunders on Lando Norris's contract extension with McLaren, confirmed until "at
least" the end of 2030 and announced in person at the McLaren Technology Centre.
Verified independently: Norris's previous deal ran through 2027, so this is a real
multi-year jump, not a routine renewal. Saunders' own follow-up is the sharper point —
McLaren locking down Norris follows the same pattern Red Bull already set with
Verstappen and Ferrari with Leclerc, meaning all of what he calls the "Big 4" teams now
have their reigning drivers tied down into the next decade. The real economic
consequence, in his words: the route into a top team for any driver not already inside
one of them "looking incredibly difficult now" — a seat-scarcity story as much as a
contract story.
