Mat Coch — PlanetF1's editor for "the politics, finance and commercial machinery of F1"
— on Woody Johnson (New York Jets owner) buying into Aston Martin. Verified
independently: Johnson takes a minority stake and joins as vice-chairman; company
filings put his formal holding at just over 1%, though the real figure may be larger
given typical lag in ownership paperwork. Lawrence Stroll's Yew Tree Consortium keeps
control; Arctos Partners and Adrian Newey are also minority shareholders. Coch's own
framing is the sharpest part: the deal shows "how the company has been structured and
its value gradually released — all without giving up control," a real ownership-
engineering story that's happening while the team sits P10 on track with Stroll and
Alonso collecting one point combined this season. Value and results moving in opposite
directions is exactly the kind of gap PaddockIntel's economic lens exists to explain.
