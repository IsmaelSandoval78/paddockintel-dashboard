Luke Smith on Lando Norris co-founding LN4 Fusion, a junior single-seater team launched
ahead of the 2027 season — announced at an event near Monza while Norris is the reigning
F1 world champion. Verified independently: the team has acquired assets from AIX Racing
to enter Formula 3, FRECA, and Italian F4 immediately, and has begun the application
process for Formula 2. Rene Rosin (ex-Prema) is Team Principal; the operation is based
in Veneto, Italy with a UK arm in Cambridgeshire. Norris has also launched a separate
LN4 Legacy Programme to fully fund promising young drivers early in their careers. A
reigning champion building the pathway infrastructure underneath himself, not just
racing on Sundays — the kind of driver-as-business-owner story PaddockIntel's economic
lens exists to cover.
