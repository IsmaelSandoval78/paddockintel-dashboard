Dieter Rencken's tribute to Bob Fernley, Force India's de facto team principal from
2008 until the team's 2018 sale to Lawrence Stroll (owner Vijay Mallya was largely
hands-off). Under a budget dwarfed by the midfield's bigger spenders, Fernley's team
still finished P4 in the constructors' championship in both 2016 and 2017 — the exact
kind of per-dollar competitive efficiency PaddockIntel covers. Rencken's line — "one of
the few who fully understood the F1 business" — from someone who has spent decades
covering the sport's finances, is a real credibility signal on its own.
