Joe Saward marking Lando Norris's win at Zandvoort with the real story underneath it —
2026 was the Dutch GP's last appearance on the F1 calendar. Confirmed independently:
Zandvoort's promoter agreed only to a one-year extension back in December 2024 rather
than a multi-year deal, and ultimately chose not to continue past 2026 despite F1
offering alternatives (alternation, annual slot) — the stated reason is financial, not
sporting: unlike many of F1's newer venues, Zandvoort operates without government
backing or sovereign wealth support. Portimão returns in 2027 as the replacement. A
sell-out, fan-favourite race leaving the calendar purely on economics is exactly the
kind of structural story PaddockIntel exists to explain, from a veteran who's covered
nearly 700 Grands Prix and has no reason to sensationalize it.
