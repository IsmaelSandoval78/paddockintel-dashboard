Jenson Button on-record denying he's lined up to replace Martin Brundle on Sky's F1
commentary, after an account claimed a "major broadcasting change" was coming in 2026.
What makes this pick more than a denial: X's own Community Note, attached directly to
the viral claim, adds the real context — Brundle has covered roughly 16 of the season's
races for years and plans to keep doing so in 2026, down only slightly from 18 in 2025,
not the step change the original post implied; Brundle himself called the framing
"utter clickbait nonsense." A real driver's response combined with a crowd-sourced
correction of a manufactured succession story, on the record from both sides.
