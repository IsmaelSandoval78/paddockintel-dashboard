Chris Medland on Colton Herta falling short of a Super Licence bonus point during
Italian GP FP1 at Monza, after not clearing the 100km-in-session threshold, plus a
heavy F2 crash at the second Lesmo earlier the same day. The rule behind the tweet:
an FIA Super Licence applicant on the Free Practice pathway earns one bonus point per
event for completing at least 100km in FP1, capped at 10 points total toward the
40-point minimum. Herta is Cadillac's 2026 F1 test driver, racing a parallel Formula 2
season with Hitech specifically to build both his experience and his points tally —
Cadillac's own program grants him four FP1 outings this season for exactly this
purpose, and he started the year five points short of the 40 he needs. A real, rarely
explained mechanic of how a driver actually earns the right to race in F1.
