# Decision 2026-10-11 — Home page "Telemetry at Dusk" (dark, warm) — trial on the home only

**Status:** proposed (home only, preview branch). Decide on site-wide adoption after review.

## Context
- PRODUCT.md currently states that dark mode is not offered and positions PaddockIntel as "the only light-substrate F1 property".
- The authoritative design system is Swiss Industrial Print v0.3.0 (light, #F4F4F0, terracotta, Archivo Black, JetBrains Mono, Lora).
- A dark navy "Data Mode" was tested earlier and reverted.
- Ismael finds the current home too flat. PaddockIntel's best-performing videos (YouTube Shorts) use a dark, warm
  "night telemetry" look, and after a structured inspiration review (Oct 11) he explicitly chose to revisit a dark look
  for the home: graphite + sunset warm light + data drawn as terrain.

## Decision
Build the home page in the "Telemetry at Dusk" look as a scoped trial:
- Only the home route changes; all other pages keep Swiss Industrial Print.
- It is warm (sunset over graphite, terracotta inside the gradient), not the reverted navy "Data Mode".
- Team colours are reserved for data.

## Consequences
- PRODUCT.md's "only light-substrate" positioning is now under review; update it only if the trial is adopted site-wide.
- Visual continuity between the website and the video channels.
- New per-race highlights data is needed (see HANDOFF.md).
