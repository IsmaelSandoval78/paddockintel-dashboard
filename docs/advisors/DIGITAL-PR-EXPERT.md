# PaddockIntel — Digital PR / Link-Building Expert (.md advisor)

Read this before any outreach pitch goes out, before syndicating a piece elsewhere, and
monthly for authority-growth tracking. EEAT-EXPERT.md measures whether authority signals
exist (backlinks, brand mentions); this advisor owns actually going out and earning them.
Authority does not accumulate by publishing good content and waiting — someone has to put the
citable asset in front of the person who can link to it. That someone follows this checklist.

---

## Non-negotiables (fail = do not do it)

- [ ] Zero paid backlinks, zero link farms, zero "guest post for a link" networks — this is
      both a Google penalty risk and the fastest way to undo everything EEAT-EXPERT.md is
      trying to build. Authority that was bought isn't authority, and it's detectable
- [ ] No reciprocal link-exchange schemes ("link to us, we'll link to you") that exist only to
      manipulate rankings — a link earns its place because the content is genuinely useful to
      the linking page's readers, not because of a deal
- [ ] Every outreach pitch centers a real citable asset (a methodology page, a proprietary
      metric, a dataset) — never "please link to our homepage," always "here's the specific
      page that answers the specific thing you're writing about"
- [ ] Syndicated content always carries a self-referencing canonical back to the PaddockIntel
      original (see SEO-EXPERT.md's canonical rule) — syndication that doesn't credit the
      source in a way Google respects isn't distribution, it's giving away the authority

## Outreach strategy

- **Target list** (update as the landscape shifts — cross-check against
  SEO-EXPERT.md's competitive positioning section so this isn't a duplicate list):
  - RACER.com (DA 75) — authority target, not a content-angle competitor
  - Feedspot F1 Magazines directory — inclusion target for distribution
  - The Paddock Magazine — closest positioning competitor; a comparison/rebuttal piece with
    real data is a legitimate angle, disparaging the outlet or its writers is not (see
    SPORTS-JOURNALISM-EXPERT.md's competitive posture rule)
  - F1 podcasts/newsletters that cite stats on-air — a HARO-style relationship (you're the
    fast, sourced answer to "does anyone have a number for X") beats a cold pitch
- [ ] Every pitch is personalized to what that specific journalist/outlet actually covers —
      a mail-merge blast is worse than no outreach, it burns the relationship for the real
      pitch later
- [ ] Respect deadlines and embargoes when pitching around a live race weekend — being fast
      and wrong to a journalist on deadline costs the relationship permanently

## Distribution vs. link building (don't conflate them)

- Social shares, retweets, and newsletter mentions are distribution — useful, but they are
  not backlinks and don't move the authority signal EEAT-EXPERT.md tracks. Don't report a
  viral post as if it were a citation
- A genuine unlinked brand mention (someone naming "PaddockIntel" without a link) is a
  half-won battle — see EEAT-EXPERT.md's mention-tracking. This advisor owns the follow-up:
  a polite, specific ask to convert the mention into a link, not a mass "we noticed you
  mentioned us" campaign

## Measurement (monthly, not just when chasing a specific target)

- [ ] Track referring domains growth, not just the DA of the 2-3 named targets — a rising
      count of smaller, relevant F1 sites linking in is a healthier signal than one big win
- [ ] Track brand mentions (linked and unlinked) alongside backlinks — both are EEAT signals,
      see EEAT-EXPERT.md's quarterly self-audit
- [ ] Audit for toxic/spammy inbound links periodically — an unrequested link from a link farm
      still needs a disavow decision, silence is not a strategy here

## When this advisor should block outreach

Any pitch offering payment or a reciprocal link for placement, any syndication without a
canonical back to the original, or any outreach that misrepresents PaddockIntel's data or
scope to get a link — hold it: `[OUTREACH-HOLD: reason]`.
